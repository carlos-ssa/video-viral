Three scenes contain concrete actions, intended framing, camera/subject relationship and transition or continuity decisions. A dolly/tracking move is distinguished from a digital crop or lens zoom; lens, trajectory and timing are declared choices or estimates, not measurements from an unseen still. The plan preserves style, scale, axis, lighting and subject identity across scenes, proposes blockout/style frames plus temporal preview and deterministic seeking, and keeps unavailable motion inspection pending.
